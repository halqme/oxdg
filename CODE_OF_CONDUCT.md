# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

oxdg should be a welcoming place to report bugs, propose changes, review code, and discuss technical decisions.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good faith, but do not use that assumption to dismiss the impact of harmful behavior.

Acceptable participation includes:

- discussing ideas and disagreements on their technical merits;
- giving actionable feedback without attacking the person who wrote the code;
- respecting different levels of experience and different communication styles;
- correcting mistakes openly and moving the discussion forward.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.

For a conduct concern that should not be handled publicly, contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub channel. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), while keeping the project policy concise and specific to oxdg.
